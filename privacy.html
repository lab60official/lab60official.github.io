🔒 LAB60 Auto-Publisher - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At LAB60, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our application ("App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal user data.

Our App is designed purely as an automated content creation and publishing tool for the LAB60 educational channel. It does not:

  • Request or store your name, email, or phone number
  • Track your location or device information
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Collect analytics about user behavior

2. What Information Does the App Access?

The App only accesses the following via secure environment variables (.env file) on the local server:

  • TikTok API Access Token – to publish videos to the LAB60 TikTok account
  • Third-party API Keys (DeepSeek, ElevenLabs, Pexels) – for script generation, voiceover, and stock footage

Important: All API keys are stored locally on our server and are never transmitted or shared externally except through the official APIs for their intended purpose.

3. How Is Your Data Used?

  • Content Creation: AI services (DeepSeek, ElevenLabs, Pexels) generate scripts, voiceovers, and video clips based on predefined educational topics.
  • Content Publishing: The TikTok API is used to post the final video to the LAB60 TikTok account.

No data is ever sold, rented, or shared with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention

  • Logs: System logs are stored temporarily for debugging and maintenance purposes. Logs are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Generated Videos: Final video files are stored locally on the server for reference. Users can delete them at any time.
  • API Keys: Stored only in the local .env file. They are not stored in any database or external system.

5. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • DeepSeek – AI script generation. See their Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs – AI voice synthesis. See their Privacy Policy.
  • Pexels – Stock video footage. See their Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok – Video publishing. See their Privacy Policy.

We do not share your information with these services beyond the data required for their core functionality (e.g., sending a script to DeepSeek for text generation).

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. If you have concerns, you can:

  • Revoke the App's TikTok access anytime via TikTok's account settings.
  • Delete all locally stored logs and video files from the server.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
